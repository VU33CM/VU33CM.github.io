How to Get Your Amateur Radio License in India

Complete step-by-step guide for ASOC Restricted (VU3) and General (VU2) grades under the Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024.

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen
  • Minimum 12 years of age
  • No criminal record

License Grades

Grade Callsign Exam Power Privileges
Restricted VU3 Written only Lower power (typically 10–50 W)
General VU2 Written + Morse Code (8 wpm) Higher power (up to 400 W on HF)

Step-by-Step Process

Register on Saral Sanchar

Go to saralsanchar.gov.in → Registration → Select “Individual (WPC Exams/Certificates)”. Use PAN (or parent’s PAN if minor), mobile & email. Verify OTPs and note your login credentials.

Apply for the ASOC Examination

Login → WPC Apply Now → Apply for Certificates → Amateur – Apply Now. Fill details, upload photo, signature, DOB proof and address proof. Select exam centre and grade. Pay exam fee via Bharatkosh (₹100 per grade).

Appear for the Examination

Restricted: Written test (Basic Electronics + Radio Regulations) – 1 hour.
General: Written test (2 hours) + Morse Code sending & receiving at 8 words per minute.
Minimum passing mark: 40% overall.

Apply for the License

After passing, apply within 2 years. Login again → Apply for Amateur License. Fill station & equipment details, upload required documents and pay license fee (₹1,000 for 20 years or ₹2,000 for lifetime up to age 80).

Receive Your Digital ASOC

After scrutiny, WPC issues the digital certificate with your callsign (you can suggest 3 preferences). Download it from the portal. You are now a licensed Radio Amateur!
