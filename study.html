Study Material for ASOC Exam

Syllabus overview, Morse training and recommended resources for Restricted & General grades.

Exam Pattern

  • Restricted Grade: Written test only (Basic Electronics + Radio Regulations) – 1 hour
  • General Grade: Written test (2 hours) + Morse Code at 8 words per minute (sending & receiving)
  • Minimum pass mark: 40% overall (as per 2024 Rules)

Key Topics – Radio Theory

  • Elementary electricity, magnetism, Ohm’s law, resistors, capacitors, inductors
  • Alternating current, transformers, resonance
  • Semiconductor devices, diodes, transistors
  • Power supplies, rectifiers
  • Receivers (TRF & Superheterodyne) and transmitters
  • Propagation, ionosphere, antennas
  • Additional for General: modulation, satellite basics, TV/facsimile principles

Radio Regulations

  • Telecommunications Act, 2023
  • Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024
  • ITU Radio Regulations (emissions, frequency allocation, distress, identification, amateur stations, call signs)

Morse Code Training (General Grade)

Target: solid 8–12 WPM receiving and sending.

Recommended Methods

  • Koch Method – Start with 2 characters at full speed, add one only after 90% accuracy
  • Farnsworth Method – Full-speed characters with extra spacing

Free Practice Tools

  • LCWO.net (Learn CW Online)
  • G4FON Koch CW Trainer
  • IZ2UUF Morse Koch CW (Android)
  • Morse Mania app
  • Morse Code Ninja (YouTube / audio courses)

Practice both receiving and sending every day for best results.
