Ham Radio Nets in India

Popular HF and VHF nets for check-ins, rag-chewing and emergency traffic. Timings are in IST and may vary.

Why Join Nets?

  • Mark your presence on air
  • Meet other operators and exchange information
  • Check propagation and equipment performance
  • Practice operating procedures
  • Pass or receive messages (including emergency traffic)
