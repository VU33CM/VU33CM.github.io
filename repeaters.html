VHF / UHF Repeaters in India

Selected active repeaters. Always confirm current status with local hams or the latest lists maintained by VU2JOS / NIAR.

Why Repeaters Matter

Repeaters greatly extend the range of handheld and mobile radios, enable local nets, and support emergency communication. Most Indian 2m repeaters use a –600 kHz shift.

Repeater Etiquette (Quick Tips)

  • Listen first – make sure the frequency is clear
  • Use the correct shift and any required CTCSS/DCS tone
  • Keep transmissions reasonably short
  • Identify with your callsign
  • Be polite and helpful to new operators
