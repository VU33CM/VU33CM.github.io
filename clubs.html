Major Ham Radio Clubs in India

Join a local club for training, technical help, nets and friendship.

National Level

  • Amateur Radio Society of India (ARSI) – National society, IARU member, QSL bureau, advocacy. Website: arsi.info
  • National Institute of Amateur Radio (NIAR) – Hyderabad. Training, study material, technical support. Website: niar.org

Prominent Clubs & Groups

  • Indian Institute of Hams (IIH) – Bengaluru (VU2IIH)
  • Bangalore Amateur Radio Club – Bengaluru
  • Mumbai Amateur Radio Institute / MARS – Mumbai
  • Gujarat Institute of Amateur Radio (GIAR) – Gandhinagar
  • South India Amateur Radio Society – Chennai area
  • Disaster Amateur Radio Emergency Services (DARES) – Mumbai
  • Pune HAMs and Amateur Radio Club (PHARC) – Pune
  • Mangalore Amateur Radio Club (MARC) – Mangaluru
  • West Bengal / Kolkata clubs – Multiple active groups
  • Amateur Radio Society of Assam and other state societies

Benefits of Joining a Club

  • Guidance for licensing and Morse training
  • Access to club stations and antennas
  • Regular nets and technical meetings
  • Participation in Field Day, contests and public service events
  • Friendship and mentoring from experienced operators
