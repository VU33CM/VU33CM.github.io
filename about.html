About This Website & VU33CM

Manoj C M – VU33CM

This website is created and maintained by Manoj C M, amateur radio operator with callsign VU33CM.

The aim is to provide a clean, practical and up-to-date resource for Indian amateur radio enthusiasts — especially newcomers who want to obtain their ASOC license and explore the many facets of the hobby.

What You Will Find Here

  • Step-by-step licensing procedure (Saral Sanchar)
  • Study material and Morse code training guidance
  • Overview of HF, VHF, FT8, DMR, EchoLink and other modes
  • Importance of repeaters and QSL cards
  • Selected repeater and net information
  • Major Indian ham clubs and useful links

Disclaimer

Information on this site is compiled from official WPC/DoT documents, NIAR, ARSI and community sources. Rules and frequencies can change. Always cross-check the latest information on the official Saral Sanchar portal and WPC notifications before applying or operating.
