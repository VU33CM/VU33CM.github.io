Why Amateur Radio?

More than a hobby — a service, a science and a global community of experimenters.

📡

Emergency Communication

When mobile networks fail during disasters, hams provide critical communication support to authorities and the public.

🌍

Global Friendship

Talk to operators across India and the world using HF, satellites, EchoLink or digital modes — no internet required.

🔬

Technical Learning

Build antennas, experiment with radios, learn electronics, propagation and modern digital communication.

🏆

Awards & Contests

Chase DXCC, join contests, activate Parks on the Air (POTA) or Summits on the Air (SOTA) and collect QSL cards.

About HAM Corner

This website is created and maintained by Manoj C M, VU33CM as a free, modern resource for Indian amateur radio enthusiasts and aspiring hams. Information is compiled from official WPC/DoT sources, NIAR, ARSI and active Indian operators.
