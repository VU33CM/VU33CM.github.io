What is Amateur Radio?

Amateur radio is much more than talking into a microphone. It combines communication, electronics, radio science, experimentation, emergency communication and friendship.

🚨

Emergency Communication

When normal communication systems fail, amateur-radio operators can provide independent communication links using portable radios, batteries and antennas.

🌍

Global Friendship

Make contacts with people around India and the world, exchange signal reports, participate in DX and discover different cultures through radio.

🔧

Technical Learning

Learn electronics, RF circuits, antennas, propagation, digital communication, satellites and radio-frequency engineering through practical experimentation.

🏆

Awards & Contests

Take part in contests, DX awards, POTA, SOTA and many other activities that make amateur radio challenging and exciting.

🚨 Emergency Communication

Amateur radio can become especially valuable when conventional communication infrastructure is damaged or unavailable. Operators can establish temporary communication networks using radios, antennas and independent power sources.

This is one of the reasons amateur radio is an important technical and community service. Operators learn how to communicate from locations where mobile networks or internet connections may not be available.

  • Portable and field communication
  • Battery and solar-powered operation
  • VHF/UHF local communication
  • HF long-distance communication
  • Emergency communication networks

🌍 Talk Across the World

Imagine sitting at your radio station in India and hearing a station from another continent. That is the magic of DX.

Depending on frequency, propagation and antenna conditions, amateur-radio operators can communicate across cities, countries, oceans and sometimes around the entire planet.

  • DX communication
  • HF propagation
  • QSL cards and electronic confirmations
  • International amateur-radio friendship
  • Contesting and awards

🔧 Learn Radio Technology

Amateur radio provides a practical way to understand electronics and communication engineering.

You can start with a simple handheld radio and gradually explore antennas, SWR, RF amplifiers, filters, oscillators, receivers, transmitters and digital communication.

  • Electricity and electronics
  • RF and radio theory
  • Transmitters and receivers
  • Antennas and feed lines
  • Propagation
  • Digital communication

📻 Explore Amateur Radio

There are many different ways to enjoy amateur radio. Choose an area below.

🌍 HF & DX

HF amateur radio can provide long-distance communication through ionospheric propagation. Operators use different bands, antennas and modes to contact stations around the world.

📡 VHF & UHF

VHF and UHF are widely used for local communication, repeaters, mobile operation, satellite contacts and community nets.

💻 Digital Radio

Digital modes such as FT8 and other data modes allow operators to communicate using computers and radio interfaces. Digital communication is particularly popular for weak-signal and DX operation.

🛰️ Amateur Satellites

Amateur satellites allow operators to communicate through space. Satellite operation introduces exciting topics such as Doppler shift, orbital passes, antenna tracking and satellite transponders.
