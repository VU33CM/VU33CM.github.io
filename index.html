Discover a World Beyond the Internet.

Amateur radio is more than pressing a button and talking into a microphone. It is the art of making connections — across cities, oceans and continents — using radio waves, antennas, electronics and imagination.

WHY HAM RADIO?

A hobby that connects people, technology and the world.

Imagine communicating with someone hundreds or thousands of kilometres away without relying on a mobile network. Imagine building your own antenna, experimenting with electronics, decoding signals from space or helping your community when conventional communication fails.

That's amateur radio.

SERVICE

When Everything Else Goes Silent.

During cyclones, floods, earthquakes and other emergencies, telephone networks and internet services can become unreliable. Amateur radio operators can establish independent communication links using radios, batteries, generators and antennas.

The ability to communicate without depending entirely on commercial infrastructure is one of the most powerful aspects of amateur radio.

  • Emergency and disaster communication
  • Portable radio stations
  • Battery and solar-powered operation
  • Communication when infrastructure fails
DX • WORLDWIDE

One Radio. One Antenna. A World of Friends.

Amateur radio can take you far beyond your neighbourhood. With the right equipment and propagation conditions, you can make contacts with operators across India and around the world.

Every contact becomes a story — a new callsign, a new country, a new friendship.

  • HF long-distance communication
  • DX hunting and rare countries
  • QSL cards and electronic confirmations
  • International amateur radio community
EXPERIMENT • BUILD • LEARN

Your Laboratory Can Be Your Radio Shack.

Amateur radio is a gateway into electronics, RF engineering, antennas, propagation, digital communications, programming and signal processing.

You don't have to be an engineer to start. Start with a radio. Learn one concept. Build one antenna. Make one contact. Then keep experimenting.

  • Antenna design and construction
  • RF and electronics experimentation
  • Propagation and radio science
  • SDR and signal analysis

HF & DX — Reach Across Continents

High Frequency amateur radio opens the door to long-distance communication. Depending on propagation, your signal can travel thousands of kilometres and reach stations on different continents.

SSB CW / Morse FT8 FT4 RTTY DX Contest QSL

VHF & UHF — The Local Radio Community

VHF and UHF are ideal for local communication, repeaters, mobile operation, emergency groups and everyday amateur radio contacts. They are also a great starting point for a new ham.

FM Repeaters APRS DMR D-STAR EchoLink Mobile Portable

Digital Radio — Where RF Meets Computing

Modern amateur radio combines radio-frequency communication with computers and software. Weak-signal digital modes can extract signals that may be difficult or impossible to copy by ear.

FT8 FT4 JS8Call DMR D-STAR WSPR APRS RTTY

Amateur Radio Beyond Earth

Yes — amateur radio can reach space. Operators can communicate through amateur satellites, track spacecraft, receive satellite telemetry and, under appropriate conditions and authorizations, experiment with Earth-space communication.

Satellites ISS APRS Satellite Tracking Telemetry Space Communication
